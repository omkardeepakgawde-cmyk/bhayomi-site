About Bhagyomi

We are a working engineer couple proving that you don't need to quit your job to live an active, adventurous, and spiritually grounded life. Between weekdays at our desks and weekends on trails, highways, and forts, we document practical blueprints so other couples can explore confidently.

Community Guidelines

Bhagyomi is dedicated to Dharmic heritage, outdoor adventure, wholesome living, and selfless seva. Everyone is welcome to follow, learn, and explore our guides. To keep this space peaceful and focused, off-topic religious debates or proselytizing are not permitted.

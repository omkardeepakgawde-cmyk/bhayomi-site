Privacy Policy & Disclosures

Effective Date: November 2026 • Applies to www.bhagyomi.com

At Bhagyomi (accessible at https://www.bhagyomi.com), the privacy of our visitors is a top priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit our website and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration to browse Bhagyomi. If you voluntarily contact us through our Contact form or register for a community event, we receive only the information you explicitly submit (such as your name, email address, and message) solely to respond to your inquiry. We never sell or rent your personal contact information to third parties.

Bhagyomi may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to Bhagyomi and/or other sites across the web.
  • Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also disable cookies through your individual browser settings.

3. Affiliate Links Disclosure

Some trekking gear, riding accessories, and kitchen product recommendations on Bhagyomi may contain affiliate links (such as Amazon Associates). If you purchase a product through these links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend gear and products we genuinely trust or use ourselves.

4. Analytics & Log Files

Bhagyomi may use standard web analytics tools (such as Google Search Console or Google Analytics) to understand aggregate visitor trends, popular guides, and device types so we can improve our content. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information.

5. External Links & Travel Disclaimer

Our guides link to external sites (such as Instagram or gear retailers) whose privacy practices we do not control. Outdoor activities such as trekking, rock climbing, and highway riding carry inherent risks — please verify current weather, trail permissions, and safety conditions before undertaking any itinerary published on this site.

6. Consent & Contact

By using our website, you consent to this Privacy Policy. If you have questions or wish to request deletion of any message submitted via our contact form, please reach out through our Contact page.

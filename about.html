About Bhagyomi — A Sanatan Yatra

Balancing full-time engineering careers with purposeful adventure, endurance sports, Dharmic heritage, wholesome home cooking, and community seva.

Who We Are

Namaste! We are Omkar and Bhagyashri — an engineer couple based in Maharashtra, India. Like millions of working professionals, our weekdays are dedicated to technical engineering work. On weekends, we step away from screens to climb Sahyadri cliffs, run marathons, cycle, ride highways, visit ancient temples, cook nutritious meals at home, and give back through fort cleanups and tree plantation drives.

We started Bhagyomi (alongside our Instagram page @bhagyomidiaries) to show that living an active, culturally rooted, and adventurous life together does not require quitting your job or spending a fortune on luxury travel.

What You Will Find on Bhagyomi.com

  • Treks & Endurance: Step-by-step itineraries, safety checklists, and budgets for Sahyadri forts, Himalayan treks, marathons, and cycling.
  • Road Trips & Rides: Practical rider-and-pillion motorcycle touring advice, car road trips, and honest public-transport travel guides.
  • Dharmic Heritage: Temple travel blueprints, fort history, and respectful guides celebrating India's Indic and Sanatan heritage.
  • Home Kitchen & Fuel: Tested home-cooked recipes and recovery meals designed for active lifestyles.
  • Seva & Community Drives: Updates and volunteer sign-ups for trail cleanups, fort restoration efforts, plantations, and charitable initiatives.

Our Editorial & Community Ethos

Every itinerary, gear note, and recipe published on www.bhagyomi.com is written from firsthand personal experience. Bhagyomi is dedicated to Dharmic heritage (Sanatan, Buddhist, Jain, and Sikh traditions), outdoor adventure, and mindful living. Readers from all walks of life are welcome to follow, learn, and explore our guides. To keep this community peaceful and focused, off-topic religious debates or proselytizing are not permitted.
